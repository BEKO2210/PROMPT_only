# AI-Assisted Methodology — Generic boilerplate

This text opens the LIMITATIONS section of every Tier-2 sellable
deliverable. It establishes that the work was produced with AI
assistance under consultant supervision.

---

## Canonical text (substitute bracketed values per engagement)

> This [audit / assessment / report / analysis] was produced by an AI
> agent on [DATE] from [analysis of the codebase / system /
> documentation / public sources] at [commit SHA / version / point
> in time]. Findings reflect the state at that point; subsequent
> changes (code, configuration, dependencies, regulatory standards,
> third-party services) may produce different conclusions. The
> producing consultant has reviewed the AI-generated output and
> takes professional responsibility for it under the engagement
> agreement, consistent with current professional practice for
> AI-assisted consulting deliverables.

## Why this language

- "AI agent" is honest disclosure. Hiding it is a growing liability
  as buyers and procurement increasingly require disclosure.
- "Has reviewed... takes professional responsibility" places
  accountability on the consultant, not the tooling.
- "Current professional practice" anchors against the 2025-26 norm
  rather than treating AI assistance as exceptional.

## Do not soften

Removing the AI disclosure to make the deliverable look more
"human-produced" creates fraud exposure. The disclosure is what
makes the methodology professionally defensible.
