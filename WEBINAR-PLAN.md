# WEBINAR-PLAN — Webinar mit Funnel (verkaufbares Deliverable)

Plans an end-to-end webinar: topic, hook, registration page copy,
agenda, slide outline, post-webinar follow-up, and evergreen reuse
(on-demand replay, gated lead magnet). Live webinars still convert
best at the top of B2B funnels in 2026.

**Realistic engagement price: €1 000 – 5 000 per webinar engagement.
Recurring as evergreen content.**

---

## How to use

Paste the block below, then on the next line:

    TOPIC: <what the webinar covers — be specific>
    AUDIENCE: <ICP — role, pain, awareness stage>
    HOST: <speaker(s) + credentials>
    GOAL: <demo bookings | trial signups | lead capture | community-building>
    LENGTH: <30 / 45 / 60 minutes (45 is the 2026 sweet spot)>
    PROOF: <data, case studies, customer quotes available>
    DATE: <when it runs>

---

## The prompt (copy from here)

```
You are producing a webinar plan. Six deliverables: §3 TITLE +
HOOK, §4 REG PAGE COPY, §5 AGENDA + SLIDE OUTLINE, §6 PROMOTION
PLAN, §7 LIVE-DAY PLAYBOOK, §8 FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE.

Three rules:

(1) TEACH ONE THING WELL. Most webinars try to teach 5 things and
    sell at the end. Best-performing webinars in 2026 teach ONE
    valuable thing thoroughly and offer the next-step naturally.
(2) THE TITLE DECIDES ATTENDANCE. Registration is everything;
    show-up rates are ~30–50% of registrations. A weak title kills
    a strong webinar.
(3) THE OFFER IS NATURAL, NOT TACKED ON. The teach naturally leads
    to the offer. Bait-and-switch teaching + hard sell at minute
    35 = unsubscribes.

1. TOPIC FRAMING
   Translate TOPIC into the audience's pain:
     - What problem does the audience have that this addresses?
     - What does success look like for them?
     - What's the ONE insight you'll deliver that they'll remember
       a week later?
   Reject if the topic isn't sharp enough — vague webinars don't
   convert.

2. ATTENDANCE THESIS
   Estimate realistic registration and attendance:
     - Cold email list of 1 000: ~5–15 registrations
     - Warm email list of 1 000 (past customers / engaged): ~30–
       80 registrations
     - Paid social ads: depends on offer + audience; €2–8 per
       registration typical B2B
     - Show-up rate: 30% (cold registrations) to 60% (warm)
   Sets expectations BEFORE running so success is measurable.

3. TITLE + HOOK  ← deliverable (a)
   3–5 title variants. Patterns that work:
     - "How <ICP> can <outcome> in <timeframe>"
     - "<Counterintuitive claim>: Why <conventional wisdom> is
       wrong"
     - "<Big number / metric>: <what the data shows>"
     - "Live teardown: <specific real example>"
   Plus the hook — the 1-sentence promise that appears on the
   registration page.

4. REGISTRATION PAGE COPY  ← deliverable (b)
   --- REG PAGE START ---
   # <Title>
   ## <Subtitle: the hook in one line>

   ### What you'll learn
   - <Specific takeaway 1>
   - <Specific takeaway 2>
   - <Specific takeaway 3>

   ### Who this is for
   <Specific role + situation — exclude as well as include>

   ### Hosted by
   <Host name, role, brief credibility statement, photo>

   ### When
   <DATE TIME with timezone + duration>

   ### What you get
   - The live session (Q&A included)
   - The replay (sent to all registrants within 24h)
   - <Lead magnet: template / checklist / framework — increases
     reg rate ~30%>

   [Register button]

   <Trust strip if applicable: logos of past attendees' companies>
   --- REG PAGE END ---

5. AGENDA + SLIDE OUTLINE  ← deliverable (c)
   Per LENGTH (45 min default):
     - 0:00–0:05 (5 min): Welcome, set expectations, capture
       engagement (poll / question to chat)
     - 0:05–0:30 (25 min): TEACH the ONE thing. Structure: problem
       → why it matters → the framework / answer → applied example
       → action items
     - 0:30–0:40 (10 min): Q&A live
     - 0:40–0:45 (5 min): Natural offer (the next-step CTA), plus
       housekeeping
   Slide outline per section with the one message per slide.

6. PROMOTION PLAN  ← deliverable (d)
   T - 21 days:
     - Email announcement to relevant lists
     - LinkedIn announcement from HOST
   T - 14 days:
     - Follow-up email
     - Partner / cross-promo asks
   T - 7 days:
     - Reminder email
     - LinkedIn post from HOST + company page
   T - 1 day:
     - "Tomorrow" email to registrants
     - LinkedIn post
   T - 1 hour:
     - "Starting in 1 hour" email + LinkedIn post
   Day of: live tweets / LinkedIn posts during the event
   Day after: replay email + start §8 follow-up

7. LIVE-DAY PLAYBOOK  ← deliverable (e)
   Pre-show (T - 30 min):
     - Tech check (audio, slides, recording, chat)
     - Co-host briefing
     - Pre-loaded poll questions
   During show:
     - Opening engagement prompt in chat
     - Mid-show poll
     - Q&A solicitation 5 min before Q&A starts
   Post-show:
     - Replay processing (typically ready within 4–24h)
     - Lead-magnet delivery automated

8. FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE  ← deliverable (f)
   See EMAIL-SEQUENCE.md for the full framework. Webinar-specific:
     - DAY 0 (post-webinar): "Thanks for attending — here's the
       replay + slides + the next step"
     - DAY 2: deeper resource related to topic (builds trust)
     - DAY 5: case study showing the outcome
     - DAY 9: direct CTA — book a call, start trial
     - DAY 14: breakup-style — "If now's not right, when?"
   Separate sequence for NO-SHOW registrants (different psychology;
   they wanted the content but missed live).

9. EVERGREEN REUSE
   After the live event:
     - On-demand version (gated landing page, ongoing lead capture)
     - Webinar broken into 3–5 short clips for social
     - Webinar transcript turned into a blog post (see SEO-CONTENT.md)
     - Slides shared on SlideShare / SpeakerDeck
     - Top 3 Q&A questions turned into FAQ content

HARD RULES
- ONE thing taught well, not five things skimmed.
- Offer at the end is natural — set up by the teaching, not
  bolted on.
- §6 promotion plan starts T-21 days minimum. Last-minute
  webinars underperform.
- §8 NO-SHOW sequence differs from attendee sequence — they
  need different first messages.
- Respond in the language of the AUDIENCE input.

TOPIC:
AUDIENCE:
HOST:
GOAL:
LENGTH:
PROOF:
DATE:
```

## What the buyer gets

A complete webinar package: title to evergreen reuse plan. Live
webinars convert at ~10–25% to next-step (demo / call / trial) when
done well — among the highest top-of-funnel conversion rates in B2B.
Single webinar can fuel 6–12 months of follow-up content.
