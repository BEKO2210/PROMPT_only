# TRUST-AUDIT — Trust-Signal-Audit (verkaufbares Deliverable)

Specialized audit of trust signals on a website: testimonials, customer
logos, security/compliance badges, founder visibility, social proof,
guarantees, contact information. Most conversion losses on first visit
happen because of trust gaps, not feature gaps — visitors who don't
trust you never read your feature list.

**Realistic engagement price: €500 – 2 500.** Quick-turnaround audit.

---

## How to use

Paste the block below, then on the next line:

    URL: <site to audit>
    AUDIENCE: <ICP — risk-tolerance varies massively by audience>
    DEAL_VALUE: <typical purchase / engagement value — higher value needs more trust>
    CLIENT: <client name>

## The prompt (copy from here)

```
You are producing a Trust-Signal audit. The §9 REPORT is the
deliverable. Three rules:

(1) Trust gaps are visible BEFORE features. A visitor who doesn't
    trust the company never reads the product page. Above-fold
    trust strip matters most.
(2) The bar scales with deal size. A €30 SaaS subscription needs
    different trust signals than a €30 000 implementation.
(3) Fabricated trust signals are catastrophic. Stolen testimonials,
    invented metrics, fake security badges trigger fraud reports.
    Audit verifies what's there is real and identifies what's
    missing.

1. SCOPE & TRUST-BAR CALIBRATION
   Per DEAL_VALUE and AUDIENCE:
     - Low-value consumer: needs minimum (logos, reviews, secure
       payment badges)
     - Mid-value B2B: needs medium (testimonials, case studies,
       founder visibility)
     - High-value B2B / enterprise: needs maximum (named customers,
       analyst quotes, compliance attestations, team page)
   Set the expected baseline for this client.

2. ABOVE-FOLD TRUST STRIP
   What's visible without scrolling on home / landing:
     - Customer logos (count, recognisability)
     - Aggregate metric headline ("Trusted by 12 000 teams")
     - Star rating + count from a credible review platform
     - "Funded by X" / "Featured in Y" tier
   If empty: critical finding.

3. TESTIMONIALS  (the core trust signal)
   Per testimonial visible on site:
     - Is the person REAL? (named, role, company, photo)
     - Does it sound human or AI-written? (specific verb, named
       outcome, sometimes a small criticism — too perfect is
       suspicious)
     - Does the company URL link out (or is the customer logo a
       link to their site, proving they exist)?
     - Are there any verbatim duplicates from competitor sites
       (plagiarism check)?
   Generic testimonials ("Great product!" from "John D.") are
   negative trust signals — better to have none.

4. CASE STUDIES
   For higher-deal-value sites:
     - Named customer?
     - Specific outcomes with numbers?
     - Reproducible methodology?
     - Quote from a real role at the customer (LinkedIn-verifiable
       if possible)?
     - Length appropriate (long enough to feel substantive, short
       enough to read)?
   Missing case studies on a B2B site selling above €5k = critical
   gap.

5. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE BADGES
   Audit every claim:
     - SOC 2 logo → does the client have a real Type II report?
     - ISO 27001 logo → cert number visible? cert body listed?
     - GDPR compliance claim → real DPA? privacy policy current?
     - PCI logo → SAQ level appropriate? AOC available?
     - HIPAA logo → real BAA capability?
     - "Bank-level security" / "256-bit encryption" → vague claim
       or specific?
   FALSE COMPLIANCE CLAIMS ARE FRAUD. Flag prominently.

6. FOUNDER / TEAM VISIBILITY
   - About / Team page exists?
   - Real people with real names, photos, titles?
   - LinkedIn-verifiable?
   - Founder accessible (Twitter handle, blog, podcast appearances)?
   - "Hire me" / "Talk to a human" feels real, not chatbot-mediated?
   For B2B sales: hidden teams are a yellow flag; ghost teams are
   a red flag.

7. CONTACT INFORMATION
   - Real street address (jurisdictionally required in EU
     Impressum, but also a trust signal globally)
   - Real phone number (not just a chatbot)
   - Real support email (not just a form)
   - Response-time commitment
   - Company registration number / VAT ID visible
   - Legal entity matches payment processor

8. GUARANTEE & RISK REVERSAL
   - Free trial / money-back / cancel-anytime visible?
   - Terms clear (no "*at our discretion" fine print)
   - Visible at conversion points (not just deep in legal)

9. REPORT  ← the deliverable
   --- REPORT START ---
   # Trust Audit — <Client>
   **Date:** <…>   **Deal-value tier:** <low / mid / high>

   ## Executive Summary             (≤250 words: trust posture
                                     vs deal-tier expectation, top
                                     5 gaps, fraud-risk findings if
                                     any)
   ## Above-Fold Trust Strip        (§2)
   ## Testimonials                  (§3)
   ## Case Studies                  (§4)
   ## Security & Compliance         (§5 — surface any unsupported
                                     claims prominently)
   ## Founder / Team Visibility     (§6)
   ## Contact Information           (§7)
   ## Guarantees & Risk Reversal    (§8)
   ## Quick-Win Recommendations     (changes deliverable this week)
   ## Strategic Recommendations     (longer-term: collect more
                                     testimonials, pursue SOC2,
                                     publish case studies)
   ## Limitations                   (§10, verbatim)
   --- REPORT END ---

10. LIMITATIONS & DISCLAIMER  (mandatory, verbatim with substitutions)
    "This audit was produced by an AI agent on <date> from analysis
     of <URL> as visible publicly. Trust-signal authenticity (real
     customers, real compliance certifications, real team) is
     assessed where externally verifiable; some signals require
     client confirmation. Findings flagging potentially false
     compliance claims are based on visible evidence; the client
     should verify and either substantiate or remove such claims
     before further publication. Trust signals legally required in
     specific jurisdictions (EU Impressum, US BBB, UK Companies
     House details) are flagged but specific compliance with each
     jurisdiction's rules requires legal review."

HARD RULES
- §5 false-compliance findings are surfaced in the Executive
  Summary, not buried — this is a fraud-risk class.
- Every testimonial assessment cites the actual content visible on
  the page, not generic critique.
- Quick-win recommendations are things the client can do this
  week (rewrite a section, add a logo, surface an existing
  guarantee). Strategic recommendations are longer programs
  (collect 10 case studies, pursue cert).
- Respond in the language of the AUDIENCE input.

URL:
AUDIENCE:
DEAL_VALUE:
CLIENT:
```

## What the buyer gets

A focused 10–15 page audit with a quick-wins list (publishable this
week) and a strategic-recommendations list. For B2B sites selling
above €5k, this is often the single highest-conversion-lift audit
type — trust gaps tend to kill more deals than feature gaps.
