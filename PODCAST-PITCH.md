# PODCAST-PITCH — Podcast-Auftritt-Pitch (verkaufbares Deliverable)

Pitches a guest to relevant podcasts: target podcast research,
customised pitch per show, talking-point sheet, and an episode-topic
proposal that matches the show's existing format. Most "PR via
podcast" outreach fails because of generic pitching; this fixes that.

**Realistic engagement price: €500 – 2 000 per podcast outreach
batch. Or part of personal-brand retainer.**

---

## How to use

Paste the block below, then on the next line:

    GUEST: <name, role, company, credentials, claim to fame>
    TOPIC: <what they can talk about with authority>
    TARGET_PODCASTS: <list of podcasts to pitch — or "find for me">
    PROOF: <prior media, books, talks, original work>
    GOAL: <what guest wants from appearance — book sales, leads, awareness>

---

## The prompt (copy from here)

```
You are pitching a guest to podcasts. Three deliverables: §6
PITCH per podcast, §7 TALKING POINTS, §8 EPISODE PROPOSAL.

Three rules:

(1) MATCH THE SHOW'S FORMAT. Each podcast has a specific
    format — interview, debate, story-driven, panel, tactical.
    Pitch a TOPIC that fits THEIR format, not yours.
(2) LISTEN TO THE SHOW FIRST. Reference 1–2 specific episodes
    by name. Generic "love your work" pitches sort to delete.
(3) MAKE THE HOST'S JOB EASIER. Suggest the episode title.
    Suggest the questions. Bring the data / hot take / story
    they can't get from another guest.

1. PODCAST RESEARCH  (per target show)
   - Show name + host name
   - Format (solo interview / co-hosted / panel / debate / story)
   - Average episode length
   - Audience (industry, role, size, geography)
   - Recent guests (last 5–10) — pattern: who do they have on?
   - Recent topics — gap your guest can fill
   - 2–3 specific recent episodes (titles + dates)
   - Booking process visible? (form / specific contact / agent)

2. GUEST FIT  (per show)
   Why this guest, this show, this moment:
     - Audience alignment (guest's expertise ↔ show's listener
       problem)
     - Topic uniqueness (something the show hasn't covered yet
       or hasn't covered with this perspective)
     - Authority signal (PROOF that makes guest credible to this
       audience)
     - Timing (a relevant news angle, milestone, or release)

3. EPISODE TOPIC PROPOSAL
   Not "I'd like to come on your show". Instead: a specific
   episode topic the show would actually publish.
   Pattern:
     - Episode title (sounds like one of theirs)
     - 1-paragraph episode pitch (what would be discussed,
       structured as a problem the audience has)
     - 3–5 talking points / chapters
     - Hot take or counter-conventional angle (drives engagement)
     - Story / case the guest can share

4. TALKING POINTS SHEET  (for the guest)
   When booked, the guest needs to land their messages cleanly:
     - 3–5 core points the guest wants the audience to take away
     - 2–3 stories that anchor each point
     - 1–2 quotable lines (memorable, tweetable)
     - 2–3 questions the guest WANTS to be asked
     - Awkward-questions prep (what could go wrong + how to
       handle)
     - The CTA the guest wants (book / newsletter / community /
       service)

5. ASSETS READY  (for production)
   - Guest bio (short, medium, long versions)
   - Hi-res headshot
   - Title and credentials as guest wants them displayed
   - Links to drop in show notes (website, social, current
     project)
   - Sample previous appearances (if any) demonstrating audio
     quality + presentation

6. PITCH EMAIL  ← deliverable (a) — per podcast
   --- EMAIL START ---
   **Subject:** Episode idea: <one-line topic that matches their
   format>

   Hi <host first name>,

   I've been listening to <SHOW>, especially <SPECIFIC EPISODE
   TITLE + DATE>. The point about <SPECIFIC OBSERVATION FROM
   EPISODE> was sharp — and got me thinking about a story I
   think would fit your show.

   **Episode idea: "<EPISODE TITLE>"**

   <2-sentence pitch: the topic, why it matters to YOUR audience,
   what makes it a good episode.>

   Why I think this fits:
   - <Why YOUR audience cares>
   - <What's new / contrarian about this take>
   - <The guest's authority on it — specific PROOF>

   Possible talking points / chapters:
   1. <point 1>
   2. <point 2>
   3. <point 3 — ideally a hot take that drives discussion>

   The guest: <GUEST name>, <one-line role + claim to fame>.
   <PROOF link, prior media, book, talk>.

   Available <date range>; can record 30 / 45 / 60 min per your
   format. Happy to do a 15-min pre-call if useful.

   <signature>
   --- EMAIL END ---

7. TALKING POINTS  ← deliverable (b) — shared with guest pre-show
   The §4 structure as a one-page guest-prep doc.

8. EPISODE PROPOSAL  ← deliverable (c) — formal proposal if asked
   Expanded version of §3 with structure suitable for the show's
   booking process (some podcasts require a formal proposal).

HARD RULES
- Reference TWO specific episodes by title + date per pitch.
  Generic "love your show" is filtered.
- Episode title sounds like ONE OF THEIRS — match format and
  wording style.
- Hot take / counter-conventional angle is included where guest
  can defend it. Bland topics don't get booked.
- Don't pitch shows that haven't published recently — many
  podcasts go on hiatus permanently.
- Respond in the language of the TARGET_PODCASTS.

GUEST:
TOPIC:
TARGET_PODCASTS:
PROOF:
GOAL:
```

## What the buyer gets

Per podcast: a custom pitch email that increases booking rate from
~5% (generic outreach) to ~25–40% (researched, specific). Plus a
talking-points sheet that makes the guest's appearance memorable.
