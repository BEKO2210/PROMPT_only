# SOCIAL-CALENDAR — Monatlicher Social-Media-Plan (verkaufbares Deliverable)

Generates a complete monthly social-media calendar for a brand: post
themes by week, platform-specific copy variants, optimal send times,
visual concept briefs, hashtag strategy, and engagement-response
templates. Built around "content pillars" — fewer themes posted
consistently outperforms 30 random posts.

**Realistic engagement price: €500 – 2 500 per month.** Recurring.

---

## How to use

Paste the block below, then on the next line:

    BRAND: <name, what you do, who you serve>
    PILLARS: <3–5 content themes for the month — see prompt for examples>
    PLATFORMS: <LINKEDIN | X | INSTAGRAM | TIKTOK | YOUTUBE_SHORTS | THREADS>
    VOICE: <formal / casual / playful / authoritative>
    POSTING_FREQUENCY: <e.g. 4×/week per platform>
    MONTH: <month + year>
    KEY_DATES: <product launches, events, holidays relevant to brand>

## The prompt (copy from here)

```
You are producing a monthly social-media calendar. Three
deliverables: §6 CALENDAR TABLE, §7 POST DRAFTS, §8 ENGAGEMENT
PLAYBOOK.

Three rules:

(1) Content pillars > random posts. 3–5 themes posted consistently
    over a month builds recognition. 30 disconnected posts builds
    noise.
(2) Native to platform. LinkedIn is not Twitter is not TikTok.
    Cross-posting verbatim signals "we don't care" — algorithm
    suppresses.
(3) Respond as fast as you publish. The engagement window is
    1–3 hours post-publish. Plan response capacity, not just
    posting capacity.

1. PILLAR STRATEGY
   From PILLARS input or by inference:
     - EDUCATIONAL: teach the audience something (how-to, framework,
       analysis)
     - INSIGHT: original perspective on industry topic
     - PROOF: customer story, behind-the-scenes, transformation
     - OPINION: founder POV, hot take (high engagement / high risk)
     - PRODUCT: feature, update, use-case
     - CULTURE: team, values, behind-scenes (sparingly)
   Recommended mix for B2B: 40% educational, 25% insight, 20% proof,
   10% opinion, 5% product. Adjust per brand.

2. WEEKLY THEME ROTATION
   Assign one primary pillar per week to create a rhythm:
     - Week 1: Pillar A
     - Week 2: Pillar B
     - Week 3: Pillar C
     - Week 4: Pillar A or D
   Each week has 1 anchor post + supporting posts (variations,
   responses, follow-ups).

3. PLATFORM ADAPTATION
   Per platform, what works in 2026:
     - LINKEDIN: long-form text posts (1 200–1 500 chars), single
       image OR carousel (max 10 slides), no link in post (drops
       reach — drop in first comment); native video > YouTube
       link; document upload underused
     - X / THREADS: thread > single tweet for substance; reply-
       guy game in own thread to boost engagement; image > text-
       only
     - INSTAGRAM: Reels > Feed > Stories for reach; carousels
       (≥7 slides) for save-rate; consistent grid aesthetic
     - TIKTOK: 7–21 seconds for completion; hook in first 1.5s;
       on-screen text; trending audio < original audio for own
       brand
     - YOUTUBE SHORTS: 30–60 seconds; strong hook; vertical only
   Per platform, native posting rules differ. Adapt copy length,
   tone, and format per platform — never paste-and-hope.

4. POSTING-TIME GUIDANCE
   Best-times-to-post differ by audience and timezone. Defaults
   (for primary target timezone):
     - LinkedIn: Tue/Wed/Thu 9–11 AM local
     - X: weekdays 8–10 AM and 6–9 PM
     - Instagram: Tue/Wed/Fri 11 AM–1 PM and 7–9 PM
     - TikTok: Tue/Thu/Sat 9 AM, 12 PM, 7 PM
   These are starting points — measure and adjust per platform's
   own analytics.

5. HASHTAG STRATEGY
   - LinkedIn: 3–5 hashtags, mix branded + niche + broad
     (#FinTech beats #Tech for discoverability)
   - X: 1–2 hashtags max (more dilutes click-through)
   - Instagram: 5–15 hashtags (algorithm has de-prioritised but
     still helps discovery on Reels)
   - TikTok: 3–5 hashtags including 1 trending if topical
   Branded hashtag campaign once per month max (consistency builds
   it, spam kills it).

6. CALENDAR TABLE  ← deliverable
   --- CALENDAR START ---
   # <BRAND> Social Calendar — <MONTH>

   | Date | Day | Platform | Pillar | Post Title | Format | Time | Status |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | Mon Jun 2 | 1 | LinkedIn | Educational | "5 ways…" | Text+Image | 10:00 | Draft |
   | Mon Jun 2 | 1 | X | Educational | (LinkedIn variant) | Thread | 09:00 | Draft |
   | …
   --- CALENDAR END ---

7. POST DRAFTS  ← deliverable
   For each row in §6, the actual post draft. Per post:
     - Platform-specific format and length
     - Hook (first line is everything on every platform)
     - Body
     - CTA (or non-CTA — not every post asks)
     - Hashtags (per §5)
     - Visual concept brief: what the image / video shows, on-screen
       text, format ratio
     - First-comment text (LinkedIn / Instagram if needed for link
       drop)

8. ENGAGEMENT PLAYBOOK  ← deliverable
   Per post, expected response types and prepared replies:
     - Genuine question → answer in 3–5 sentences with link to deeper
       resource if exists
     - Disagreement → acknowledge POV, restate why we believe what
       we wrote, exit if escalating
     - Brand mention by user → like + reply with thanks, save for
       repost permission ask
     - Spam / promotion → ignore (do not engage)
     - DM follow-up from post → 24h response SLA
   For LinkedIn / Instagram specifically: comment-on-others-content
   schedule (15 min/day, 3–5 thought-leader accounts in space) —
   reciprocal engagement amplifies own reach.

9. METRICS TO TRACK  (per month)
   - Reach per platform
   - Engagement rate per pillar (which pillars resonate)
   - Best-performing posts (drives next month's themes)
   - Follower growth + audience-quality (relevant titles vs
     random)
   - DM / inbound-lead volume
   - Click-through to site (UTM-tagged links)

HARD RULES
- Each platform's post is rewritten for that platform — not
  copy-paste from another.
- Character / length / hashtag rules per platform are respected.
- Visual brief is specific enough that a designer can produce the
  asset without further questions.
- Posts referencing real metrics / customer outcomes cite the
  source from a PROOF-style input.
- Respond in the language of the BRAND input.

```

executes day-by-day, plus a response playbook for the engagement
window. Sold as a recurring retainer (€500–2 500/month per brand);
add design-asset production for additional fee.
